Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 22, 2026

Welcome to Nexus Pulse ("we", "our", or "us"). Nexus Pulse operates the website at https://mahmoudsamour.github.io/nexus-pulse/ and related automated media publishing tools.

This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information in connection with our services, automated syndication tools, and third-party integrations.

1. Information We Collect

Nexus Pulse is designed as an open autonomous intelligence and media curation studio. We minimize data collection:

  • Website Visitors: We do not require account registration or collect personal identifying information to browse our daily intelligence reports.
  • Newsletter / Telegram Subscribers: If you voluntarily join our Telegram broadcast channel or subscribe to email updates, we only store the contact handle necessary to deliver requested updates.
  • Log Data: Standard anonymous web server request metrics (IP address, browser type, referral URLs) may be logged by our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) for security and operational integrity.

2. Google & YouTube API Services

Nexus Pulse uses Google OAuth and the YouTube Data API solely for automated uploading and managing verified video content produced by the studio.
  • Our application requests only the minimum necessary OAuth scopes (e.g., youtube.upload) required to publish daily video briefings.
  • We do not share, sell, or transfer your Google or YouTube account credentials to any third parties.
  • Authentication tokens are encrypted and stored locally solely for headless execution of the publishing pipeline.
  • Nexus Pulse adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

3. Third-Party Services

Our platform integrates with third-party social media and syndication APIs (including YouTube, Telegram, LinkedIn, and Reddit). When you interact with our content on those platforms, their respective privacy policies apply:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • YouTube Terms of Service
  • Telegram Privacy Policy

4. Data Retention and Security

We implement strict access controls and industry-standard operational measures to protect credentials and automated tokens. Sensitive credentials are never exposed in public repositories.
